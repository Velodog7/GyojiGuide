Sumo Slap Down — Fantasy Sumo: rikishi analysis, mock drafts, a basho simulator, and private fantasy leagues

Welcome to Sumo Slap Down! I created this site because my family and friends who love sumo and fantasy sports wanted a single destination to analyze rikishi, mock draft your team, simulate upcoming basho, and customize private fantasy leagues. The site is still in beta so please reach out with any bug reports or suggestions on how to improve the site. Hakkeyoi!

  • Analyze Wrestlers — Dig into historical stats, monitor health updates, and track performance to scout your perfect lineup.
  • Create Mock Drafts — Practice your picks and have your draft instantly graded against our predictions.
  • Run Simulations — Watch a day-to-day simulation of the entire tournament using hypothetical matchups.
  • Compete in Leagues — Join public games or create private custom fantasy leagues—including dedicated Keeper Leagues that let you build a stable and carry your top wrestlers from one basho to the next.
  • Stay Connected — All of our rikishi data and tournament results are powered by the sumo-api, ensuring your stats, rankings, and match history are always accurate and up to date.
